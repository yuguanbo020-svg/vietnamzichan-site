Expansion of Industrial Parks in Hung Yen Province, 2026

2026-08-19

Increased production space and attraction of new industrial investment.

Source facts

  • Vietnam Investment Review (VIR) reported that Hung Yen province's industrial production grew 15.4% year-on-year in the first seven months of 2026, with manufacturing and processing up 16.05%.
  • The report said Hung Yen newly established three industrial parks — Tan Dan, No. 08 and No. 10 — and added four new industrial clusters.
  • The report said that as of 31 July 2026, Hung Yen had 975 cumulative foreign-invested projects with total registered capital of US$18.8 billion, and 3,230 domestic investment projects with total registered capital of US$33.2 billion.
  • The report said a planned high-tech park in Hung Yen covers nearly 500 hectares with eight functional zones, with an estimated investment of about US$400 million.

Business implications

  • Demand for production land in Hung Yen is rising, offering investors new options.
  • New industrial parks may reduce operating costs and improve production efficiency through better planning.
  • Infrastructure expansion in industrial zones will support growth in local manufacturing.

What it means for Vietnam–China business

  • Foreign investors may shift investment to Hung Yen instead of neighboring provinces.
  • Expansion of industrial zones could enhance Vietnam's ability to attract Chinese investment.
  • High-tech projects may create opportunities for collaboration between Vietnamese and Chinese firms.

What to watch

  • The number of new investment projects in Hung Yen may increase over time, especially in manufacturing.
  • Completion progress of new industrial parks will affect future investor attraction efforts.
  • Growth trends in Hung Yen may reflect supply chain shifts from China to Vietnam.

Three scenarios

Base
If new industrial zones are completed on schedule, investment in manufacturing in Hung Yen will increase significantly.
Upside
If high-tech projects launch early and attract major investors, the region could become an attractive destination for Chinese companies.
Downside
If planning and development fail to meet expectations, investors may move to other provinces with better infrastructure.
Reference source：越南投资评论(VIR) · 2026-08-19
