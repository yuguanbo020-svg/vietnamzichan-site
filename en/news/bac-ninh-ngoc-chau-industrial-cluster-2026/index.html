Groundbreaking of Ngoc Chau Industrial Cluster in Bac Ninh

2026-08-04

The project invests nearly 1 trillion VND, covers 75 hectares, and focuses on multiple industrial sectors.

Source facts

  • The groundbreaking ceremony for the Ngoc Chau Industrial Cluster in Bac Ninh Province took place on 4 August 2026.
  • The project has a planned area of about 75 hectares and total investment of nearly VND 1 trillion.
  • Land clearance for 35 hectares in the first phase has been completed; construction, acceptance and commissioning are planned for 2027.
  • The plan targets textiles, pharmaceuticals, materials, machinery, electronics, automotive, furniture and supporting industries, as well as warehouse and factory services.

Business implications

  • Increases available industrial land for domestic and foreign companies in northern Vietnam.
  • Reduces operational costs and improves land use efficiency through centralized planning.
  • Encourages relocation of factories from neighboring provinces or China to benefit from infrastructure and incentives.

What it means for Vietnam–China business

  • Chinese firms may shift production to this area instead of investing in China.
  • The cluster could attract Chinese investors, especially in textiles and machinery industries.
  • It may become a hub for supporting industries for Chinese companies, reducing logistics costs.

What to watch

  • Construction progress in 2026 will determine when the project becomes operational.
  • Early foreign investor attraction indicates development potential of the industrial zone.
  • Tax and land policies for businesses may change over time.

Three scenarios

Base
Project completed on schedule, attracting domestic and international investors, generating jobs and boosting regional economy.
Upside
Chinese firms relocate production, bringing significant investment and labor to the area.
Downside
Delays or lack of investor interest may prevent project from meeting expectations.
Reference source：Vietnam Government News · 2026-08-04
